SALES DASHBOARD DOMAIN CONTEXT

Focus areas for sales data analysis:
- Revenue, GMV, ARR, MRR, net revenue metrics
- Sales pipeline: leads, opportunities, conversions, win rate
- Regional and territory performance breakdowns
- Product/SKU/category revenue contributions
- Time-based trends: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual
- Sales rep performance and quota attainment
- Average deal size, cycle length, close rate
- Churn rate and customer retention metrics

Preferred chart types for sales:
- KPI cards for: Total Revenue, Total Units Sold, Avg Deal Size, Win Rate %
- Bar charts for: Revenue by Region, Revenue by Product, Sales Rep Performance
- Line charts for: Monthly/Quarterly Revenue Trend, MoM Growth
- Pie/Donut charts for: Revenue by Category, Channel Mix
- Funnel charts for: Sales Pipeline (Leads → Qualified → Proposal → Closed)

When column names are ambiguous, prefer mapping:
- revenue/sales/amount → numeric metric (y_field)
- region/territory/location → dimension (x_field)
- date/month/quarter/period → time dimension (x_field)
- product/item/sku/category → categorical dimension (x_field)
- rep/agent/owner → person dimension

Always include at least one KPI card and one trend line chart in sales dashboards.
Color guidance: Use #0ea5e9 (blue) for primary metrics, #10b981 (green) for positive trend indicators.
