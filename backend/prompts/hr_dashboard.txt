HR / PEOPLE ANALYTICS DASHBOARD DOMAIN CONTEXT

Focus areas for HR data:
- Headcount: total employees, by department, by location, by level
- Attrition and turnover rate (voluntary vs involuntary)
- Hiring pipeline: applicants, interviews, offers, hires
- Tenure distribution and average tenure
- Diversity and inclusion metrics by department/level
- Compensation bands and equity analysis
- Performance ratings distribution
- Absenteeism and leave tracking
- Training completion and L&D metrics

Preferred chart types for HR:
- KPI cards for: Total Headcount, Attrition Rate %, Avg Tenure, Open Positions
- Bar charts for: Headcount by Department, Hires by Month, Attrition by Department
- Line charts for: Headcount Over Time, Monthly Attrition Trend
- Pie/Donut charts for: Gender Distribution, Employment Type Mix, Department Breakdown
- Funnel charts for: Hiring Pipeline (Applied → Screened → Interviewed → Offered → Hired)

When column names are ambiguous, prefer mapping:
- employee_id/emp_id → count metric
- department/dept/team → categorical dimension
- date_of_joining/hire_date → time dimension
- status/active/employed → filter/boolean dimension
- salary/compensation/pay → numeric metric (use for compensation analysis)
- rating/score/performance → numeric metric

Privacy note: Avoid generating charts that expose individual employee PII.
Always include a headcount KPI and department breakdown in HR dashboards.
Color guidance: Use #8b5cf6 (purple) for HR metrics to distinguish from sales/finance.
