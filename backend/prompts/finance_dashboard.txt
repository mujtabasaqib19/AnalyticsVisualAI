FINANCE DASHBOARD DOMAIN CONTEXT

Focus areas for financial data:
- P&L: Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit, EBITDA, Net Income
- Budget vs Actuals variance analysis
- Cash flow: operating, investing, financing activities
- Balance sheet: assets, liabilities, equity
- Cost center and departmental spend breakdown
- Financial ratios: gross margin %, net margin %, ROI, ROE
- Accounts receivable / payable aging
- Expense categorization and trend analysis
- YTD, QTD, MTD performance vs targets

Preferred chart types for finance:
- KPI cards for: Total Revenue, Net Profit, Gross Margin %, Cash Balance
- Bar charts for: Budget vs Actuals by Department, Monthly Expenses by Category
- Line charts for: Revenue Trend YTD, Cash Flow Over Time, Cumulative P&L
- Area charts for: Revenue vs Expenses trend (stacked areas show margin)
- Table: Detailed ledger view, aging report

When column names are ambiguous, prefer mapping:
- amount/value/budget/actual → numeric metric
- account/category/cost_center → categorical dimension
- date/period/month/quarter → time dimension
- variance → numeric metric (positive = favorable, negative = unfavorable)
- department/entity/subsidiary → categorical dimension

Always include at least one KPI card with margin % and one trend line in finance dashboards.
Color guidance: Use #10b981 (green) for positive metrics, #ef4444 (red) for negative variances, #f59e0b (amber) for budget values.
